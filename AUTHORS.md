## Licence choice

GPL rather than a permissive licence, for three reasons:

- Relicensing from GPL to a permissive licence is always possible while the
  author holds full copyright; the reverse is not. GPL keeps both options open.
- MuseScore itself is GPL-3.0, so there is no friction in that ecosystem.
- Derivative works stay open, so improvements can come back to the project.

Because copyright is held in full, dual licensing remains available: GPL for
general use, a commercial licence for anyone needing to embed the engine in
closed software.

## Contributing

A Contributor Licence Agreement is required before the first pull request is
accepted. Without one, copyright becomes shared after a few contributions and
the dual-licensing option is lost.

## Feedback data

The source is open under GPL-3.0. Any future mechanism for collecting learner
feedback — corrected boundaries, reports of misread bars — will be a separate
component, and the data it gathers belongs to the author.
